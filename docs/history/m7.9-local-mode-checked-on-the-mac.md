## Security checklist

For the fixes; each commit answers it in full.

1. **Inputs:** new, in the [inventory](../security.md#untrusted-inputs) with their bounds and
   hostile tests: `to-make.json` (an authority file, strict JSON, bounded), git's progress
   output read from a sync's fetch (unknown phases, forged `remote:` lines, overflowing counts
   refused), the sync status read (`writ admin repo status`); the guided scan's proxy answer
   (a name in force or none, escaped).
2. **Authority:** no new grant; owed keys are those a policy declares, checked at unseal
   against the policy whose MAC is checked; the status read is a reader's.
3. **Fail closed:** a device key that won't sign is unusable and named, never replaced by a
   software key; an approval with an unusable CA refused with its code, the ticket kept; an
   owed key a device refuses stops the start, the entry kept.
4. **Audit:** one `key` line per key made at start, one per unusable key at unseal with what
   replaces it, one for a TLS key refused at start.
5. **Revocation:** not touched.
6. **Secrets:** `to-make.json` holds declarations only; the probe's signature is thrown
   away.
7. **Display:** owed and unusable keys named with their commands; status rows within 78
   columns.
8. **Dependencies:** none new.
9. **Guarantees:** not weakened; the device keys' tests rewritten for the key made by the
   daemon (S-10's).
10. **Threat model:** not touched.
