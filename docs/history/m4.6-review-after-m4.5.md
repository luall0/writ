## Decisions

- **A lock that serializes leases is held by the work, never by a peer.** A push's mirror goes
  with receive-pack's exit, at once on a refusal, and 10 s after an acceptance at the latest:
  a client that stops reading holds nothing. A ref update later than the grace is checked
  against its old value (as upstream's `--force-with-lease` checked it), so releasing early
  can make a racing update fail, never land past the decision.
- **Waiting for the mirror has a deadline**: the longest a holder doing its job keeps it
  (`mirrors.timeout` + 10 minutes). A caller queued behind several slow holders can be refused
  "the mirror is busy" though each behaved: fail closed with a reason, "try again".
- **`mirrors.ca_certs` replaces the system's CAs entirely**, the CA directory included; with
  no `ca_certs`, the system's, as before.
- **The ledger is read before a push's objects are walked**: what a push costs the daemon is
  bounded by what the lease signed, not by what the agent sends.
- **The daemon's git is configured** (`mirrors.git`), like its ssh: one rule for the programs
  the daemon runs.
- **Kept as is**: `Timeout` reports `mirrors.timeout` for the `ls-remote` after a fetch (that
  `ls-remote` gets the sync's remaining time, so its timeout is the sync's); `Gateway::start`
  (65 lines) and `push_upstream` (62) within the convention's "about 60".

## The review loop

After the seven steps, the diff since M4.5 was reviewed again for security, by hand, until a
pass found nothing.

**Pass 1** (the whole diff):

- **F1, a report larger than the pipe kept the mirror.** receive-pack moves the refs, then
  writes its report before exiting; with up to 1,000 refs and long branch names, a report
  larger than the pipe, unread by a client with its window shut, kept receive-pack from
  exiting, so step 1's release at exit never came. Fixed: the hook's yes starts
  `REF_UPDATE_GRACE` (10 s), after which the mirror is released anyway.
- Looked at and kept: the background sync logs a busy mirror and goes on to the next; the
  ledger lookups are indexed (the primary key starts with the commit id) and are the ones
  `Service::push` already made; the refactor keeps every check, its order, every message and
  audit line.

**Pass 2** (F1's fix, and every holder of the mirror):

- **F2, a refused push held the mirror the same way**: receive-pack writes a report for a
  refusal too (an `ng` line per ref). Fixed: a refused push moves no ref, so its mirror is
  released as soon as it is refused (`after_decision`).

**Pass 3** (F2's fix, the holders again): nothing found. A sync is bounded by `mirrors.timeout`
and the repack; a decision by the lock's deadline, the read's and the upstream push's, then
the mirror goes at once (refused), at the grace or at the exit (accepted); every error before
the guard is taken takes none; a client gone mid-decision leaves receive-pack waiting on a
bounded hook; marking a mirror stale holds it for an instant. The loop ends here.

## The security checklist, for M4.6

1. **Inputs**: a push's channel can no longer hold the mirror past its command (S1, F1, F2);
   what a push costs is bounded by what its lease signed (S4); arithmetic on what agents and
   upstream send is checked by lint (Q3); `mirrors.git` is an absolute path checked at load
   and at startup.
2. **Authority**: `mirrors.ca_certs` now decides alone, as it was documented to (S2).
3. **Fail closed**: a mirror busy past its deadline refuses with a reason; a CA directory that
   can't be emptied, or a git that isn't an executable file, stops the start; a ledger read
   that fails refuses the push.
4. **Audit**: every new refusal (busy, the read's deadline) is logged like any gateway refusal.
5. **Revocation**: not touched.
6. **Secrets**: not touched (the token is still taken out of what upstream says).
7. **Display**: a certificate refused by GnuTLS says so (S3).
8. **Dependencies**: none added (tokio's `test-util` for `writ-integration`'s tests only).
9. **Guarantees**: "an HTTPS upstream's token goes only to the registry's host, over TLS it
   verified" gains `the_operator_s_cas_are_the_only_ones_git_is_given`; nothing weakened.
10. **Threat model**: the `mirrors.ca_certs` residual-risk row made true on Debian
    (strengthened); no new residual risk.
